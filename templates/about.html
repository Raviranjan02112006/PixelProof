PROJECT OVERVIEW

What is PixelProof?

An experimental approach to identifying AI-generated images through multiple computational techniques.

Introduction

The rapid development of generative artificial intelligence has made it increasingly easy to create realistic synthetic images. These images can sometimes be difficult to distinguish from authentic photographs through visual inspection alone.

PixelProof explores this challenge by examining different image characteristics, including frequency patterns, pixel correlations, spatial structures, statistical features and learned visual representations.

The project combines eight individual mathematical and machine-learning models into a weighted ensemble and evaluates the image separately using a trained ResNet50 Convolutional Neural Network.

MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION

Image Analysis Techniques

METHOD 01

Fourier Transform

Two-dimensional Fourier Transform is used to examine frequency-domain characteristics of an image.

Features include frequency magnitude, low-frequency energy, high-frequency energy and spectral entropy.

METHOD 02

Pixel Correlation

Correlation analysis investigates statistical relationships between neighbouring pixels.

Horizontal, vertical and diagonal correlations and local variance characteristics are examined.

METHOD 03

Spatial Analysis

Spatial features are extracted using image gradients, edge density, intensity statistics and gradient direction histograms.

METHOD 04

Relative Depth Estimation

Depth Anything V2 is used to estimate relative depth information from a single image.

The estimated depth map is used to calculate statistical depth and depth-gradient features.

The individual model outputs are combined using validation-based performance weights and weighted soft voting.

DEEP LEARNING ARCHITECTURE

ResNet50 CNN

An independent deep-learning approach for image classification.

Convolutional Neural Network

PixelProof uses a ResNet50 architecture with pretrained ImageNet weights and a custom classification head.

The model receives resized 224 × 224 RGB images and produces a classification score for REAL and FAKE categories.

Its prediction is evaluated independently from the mathematical ensemble before the two outputs are combined.

FINAL DETECTION APPROACH

Hybrid Prediction System

Two independent approaches contribute to the final classification.

01

Mathematical Ensemble

Combines eight individual model outputs through validation-based weighted soft voting.

02

ResNet50 CNN

Produces an independent deep-learning classification score for the uploaded image.

03

Final Hybrid Verdict

Combines the mathematical and CNN evidence to produce the final REAL or FAKE prediction.

RESEARCH LIMITATIONS

Understanding the Results

Important Considerations

  • Model predictions are based on learned patterns and extracted features.
  • Mathematical evidence percentages are not necessarily calibrated probabilities.
  • Relative depth estimation does not provide actual physical distance.
  • Image compression, resizing and unfamiliar image-generation methods may affect prediction performance.
  • The system provides an experimental classification, not a guarantee of image authenticity.
